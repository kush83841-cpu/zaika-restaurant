About Us

A Place Where Taste Meets Togetherness

Zaika is a modern restaurant in the heart of New Delhi, bringing together the best of Italian, Indian and Continental kitchens under one roof. From wood-fired pizzas to slow-cooked butter chicken, every dish is made fresh with handpicked ingredients.

Whether it is a family dinner, a date night or a celebration with friends, Zaika is made for moments worth remembering — good food, warm service and a space that feels like home.
